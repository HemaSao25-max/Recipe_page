INTRODUCTION :-

I love mashroom A mashroom is a type of fungus, not a plant. The part you see above groundis the fruiting body, which spreads spores to help the fungus reproduce. Edible mushrooms are a healthy source of vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants, while some wid varieties are highly poisonous

HOW TO MAKE MUSHROOM CURRY :-

INGREDIENTS :-

  • Mushroom (white buttom or cremini work well)
  • Onions, finely chopped

  • Garlic, minced or pasted.
  • Cooking oil or ghee
  • Water or vegetable stock
  • Garam masala powder
  • salt, to taste
  • Ginger, grated or pasted

INTRUCTIONS :-

    1. Prepare the cashew paste (optional): If using, soak the cashews in ¼ cup hot water for 10 minutes. Blend until you get a smooth, creamy paste and set it aside. Sauté the aromatics: Heat 2 tbsp of oil or ghee in a large pan over medium heat. Add the finely chopped onions and sauté until they turn golden brown, which should take about 9–10 minutes. Add ginger and garlic: Add the ginger and garlic to the pan. Sauté for another minute until the raw aroma disappears. 2. Sauté the aromatics: Heat 2 tbsp of oil or ghee in a large pan over medium heat. Add the finely chopped onions and sauté until they turn golden brown, which should take about 9–10 minutes.
    3. Add ginger and garlic: Add the ginger and garlic to the pan. Sauté for another minute until the raw aroma disappears
    4. Cook the spices: Stir in the turmeric, red chili powder, coriander powder, cumin powder, garam masala, and salt. Add 2 tbsp of water to prevent the spices from burning and mix well.
    5. Simmer the tomato puree: Pour in the tomato puree. Cook, uncovered, for 5–7 minutes until the mixture thickens and the oil starts to separate from the gravy.
    6.Add the cashew paste and water: If using, add the cashew paste along with ½ to ¾ cup of hot water. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer, covered, until the gravy thickens.
    7.Add the mushrooms: If you prefer a firmer texture, you can sauté the mushrooms separately in a little oil in another pan until they begin to brown. Add the prepared mushrooms to the curry and stir to combine.
    8. Finish cooking: Cover the pan and let the curry simmer until the mushrooms are tender. If the curry becomes too thick, you can add a splash more water.
    9.Garnish and serve: Add the fresh chopped coriander leaves and mix them in. Serve hot with steamed rice or flatbreads like roti or naan.
